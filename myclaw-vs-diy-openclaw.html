The Hidden Cost of DIY

DIY looks cheaper at first glance: $50-100/month vs $199/month. But the math changes when you include your time:

  • Learning the OpenClaw platform: 20-30 hours
  • Initial configuration and testing: 10-15 hours
  • Ongoing maintenance and updates: 3-5 hours/month

If your time is worth $50/hour (conservative for a business owner), that's $1,500-2,500 in setup time alone. The done-for-you service at $199/month pays for itself in recovered time within 2-3 months.

The Real Question: What Is Your Time Worth?

DIY makes sense if you enjoy the technical process AND your time is genuinely not valuable elsewhere. For most business owners, the 30+ hours you'd spend learning to configure OpenClaw would deliver far more value spent on sales, customer relationships, or product development. The $199/month isn't paying for the technology — it's paying for the expertise and freeing your time for high-value work.

Who Should Choose DIY OpenClaw

DIY is genuinely the right choice if:

  • You're a developer or have a technical background and enjoy tinkering
  • You want deep, granular control over every aspect of your agent
  • You have time to learn properly and maintain it ongoing
  • Budget is the primary constraint and you can realistically commit to learning
  • You're building AI capabilities as a core competitive skill for your business

Who Should Choose MyClaw / Done-For-You

The managed service is the right choice if:

  • You are not technical and don't want to become technical
  • You value speed to results (agent live in 48h vs 40h of self-learning)
  • Your time is better spent running your business than configuring software
  • You want a professionally configured agent from day one, not a "good enough" DIY version
  • You want ongoing support when things need updating

Quick Decision Test

Ask yourself honestly: "In the next 30 days, do I have 30+ hours available to learn a new platform?" If the answer is no — and for most business owners it is — the done-for-you service is the right choice. You can always build more internal capability later once the agent is delivering value.

MyClaw is a managed service offering for OpenClaw that handles setup, configuration, and hosting for you. It's designed for business owners who want an AI agent without dealing with any technical configuration — you describe what you need, they build and maintain it.

What is DIY OpenClaw?

DIY OpenClaw means setting up and running the OpenClaw platform yourself. You install it, configure it, connect your integrations, and manage hosting. This gives maximum control but requires technical knowledge and 20-40 hours of initial setup time.

It depends on your situation. MyClaw is better if you're not technical and value your time. DIY is better if you're comfortable with technology and want full control. For most non-tech founders, the managed service delivers faster results and better ROI when you factor in the time cost of DIY.
