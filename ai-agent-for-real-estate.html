The Lead Timing Problem in Real Estate

Real estate leads have a half-life measured in minutes, not hours. The data is unambiguous: a prospect who gets a response within 5 minutes converts at 9x the rate of one who waits 30 minutes. Wait until morning and you have essentially no chance.

The challenge is that most real estate inquiries arrive in the evening and on weekends — when you are with family, at property viewings, or simply unavailable. You cannot be everywhere at once. Your AI agent can.

What an AI Agent Does for Real Estate Agents

A well-configured AI agent handles the entire top-of-funnel process automatically:
