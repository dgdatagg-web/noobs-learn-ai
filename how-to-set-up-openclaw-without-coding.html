What You Need to Prepare Beforehand

To get the most out of your setup, prepare these before you fill in the brief:

  • Business description — What you do, who you serve, what makes you different
  • Top 20 FAQs — The questions customers ask most often, with your preferred answers
  • Pricing information — Decide upfront what the agent should say about pricing (exact, or "contact us for a quote")
  • Escalation triggers — What situations should be passed to a human? (complaints, pricing negotiations, etc.)
  • Tone guidelines — Formal, casual, friendly? Any specific phrases to use or avoid?

Pro Tip: The FAQ Document Is the Most Valuable Thing You'll Create

Spend 30 minutes writing out your 20 most common customer questions and ideal answers. This document powers your AI agent AND becomes a training resource for new staff AND improves your website's SEO. It's one of the highest-ROI tasks you can do for your business.

What Happens After Setup

After your agent goes live, expect:

  • Week 2-4: Patterns emerge — you'll see what types of questions it handles well and where it needs refinement
  • Month 2+: Continuous improvement as you feed it better answers and expand its knowledge base

The agent gets better over time as you update it with new information, new products, and refined answers.
