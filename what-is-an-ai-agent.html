What Exactly Is an AI Agent?

An AI agent is software that can understand, decide, and act on your behalf — automatically. It's not just a chatbot that follows a fixed script. It's not just ChatGPT that answers questions. An AI agent is more like a tireless digital employee who never sleeps, never calls in sick, and never forgets to follow up.

Here's the simplest way to think about it: if you gave a smart assistant a goal and they went off to achieve it without you having to manage every step — that's an AI agent.

AI Agent vs ChatGPT vs Chatbot: What's the Difference?

Tool What It Does Who Controls It
Chatbot (old-school) Follows a fixed decision tree You set every path manually
ChatGPT Answers questions when you ask You initiate every conversation
AI Agent Takes actions, follows up, completes tasks Works autonomously toward your goals

The key difference: an AI agent is proactive. It doesn't wait for you to ask it something. It watches for triggers (a new lead, an unanswered message, a form submission) and takes action automatically.

How Does an AI Agent Work? (Without the Tech Jargon)

Think of an AI agent as having three parts:

  1. Ears — It watches for triggers: a new message, a form submission, a time trigger ("every Monday at 9am")
  2. Brain — It understands context and decides what action is appropriate, based on rules you set upfront
  3. Hands — It takes action: sends a message, books a slot, updates a spreadsheet, escalates to you if needed

You don't need to understand the technology. You just need to know what you want it to do. The setup team handles the rest.
