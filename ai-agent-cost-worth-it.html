The ROI Calculation for a Typical Small Business

Let's use a real scenario. You run a service business (fitness studio, dental clinic, consulting firm, whatever):

  • You get 30 inquiries per month
  • Without an agent, you respond to 70% of them (missing 9 due to timing/capacity)
  • Your average new client value is $400
  • Conversion rate from inquiry to client: 25%

The Math

Without AI agent: 30 inquiries × 70% response rate = 21 responded × 25% conversion = 5.25 new clients/month

With AI agent: 30 inquiries × 100% response rate = 30 responded × 28% conversion (better because faster response) = 8.4 new clients/month

Difference: 3.15 extra clients/month × $400 = $1,260 additional revenue per month

Cost of agent: $199/month

Net gain: $1,061/month — 5.3x return on investment

These are conservative estimates. The actual numbers depend on your business, but the direction is clear: recovering missed leads and speeding up response times pays for the service many times over.

When Is an AI Agent NOT Worth It?

To be fair, there are situations where the ROI math doesn't work:

  • Very low inquiry volume — If you get 2-3 inquiries per month, the problem isn't response speed
  • Highly specialized services — Where every inquiry requires your unique expertise immediately (complex B2B sales, niche consulting)
  • Cash flow constraints — If $199/month is a genuine hardship, fix cash flow first
  • No digital presence — If customers only find you offline, the agent won't have much to work with

Hidden Costs to Be Aware Of

Any honest assessment of AI agent costs includes potential hidden costs:

  • Time to brief and review — You'll spend 2-4 hours upfront on the business brief and review
  • Ongoing updates — When your pricing, services, or policies change, the agent needs updating (good services include this)
  • Integration costs — Connecting to specific CRMs or custom systems may require additional setup

With a reputable done-for-you service, these are usually included in the monthly fee. Always confirm what's included before signing up.
